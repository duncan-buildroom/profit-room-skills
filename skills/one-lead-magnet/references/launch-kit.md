# Launch kit: what goes in `lead-magnet/launch-kit.md`

One file, these sections in this order. Write in the member's voice (brand profile Tone and words to avoid; if unset: plain words, short sentences, contractions, no em dashes). Use only facts, stories and proof the member gave. Never invent results, quotes or numbers. In health, legal or money niches: no outcome promises, general information only, put "General information, not advice" at the end of the post and of email 5, and never ask for personal details in a DM or an email.

## 1. Your path (one line)
`Post on [topic] > comment [KEYWORD] > DM with link > email form > 5 emails > [offer link]`

## 2. The promo post
- Hook line in the buyer's own words (use a repeated question from exercise 1).
- 3 to 6 short lines: the problem, what they will get, one proof point or story.
- Last line, exactly in this shape: `Comment [KEYWORD] and I'll send you the [magnet name].`
- No link in the post body.
- Give 2 hook options and recommend one.

## 3. The image text
What goes on the image, as short as possible: the magnet name or promise (big), the line `Comment [KEYWORD] for the [name]` (clear, high contrast, bottom half). The keyword must be on the image and in the post. Offer to make the image if an image tool is available; otherwise give the text and layout notes for Canva.

## 4. The DM
Two or three warm sentences and the link: say what they asked for, give it, add one easy question. No pitch. Write 3 versions, recommend one. Example shape: "Hi, thanks for asking. Here's the [name]: [link]. Which [movement/step/part] gives you the most trouble?" Keep the link to the email form (or the PDF link if there is no form yet).

## 5. The five emails
Spaced a day or two apart. Email 1 has the PDF link at the top. Only email 5 asks for anything.
1. Deliver: link at the top, one line on where to start inside it.
2. A real story or lesson that shows you understand the problem. Ask them to reply with how far they got.
3. Go deeper on one section. One idea they can use today.
4. Proof: a real result or review, only with permission.
5. The soft ask: "If any of this sounds like you, here's how to start." One link, the offer.
Each email: subject line (plus one alternate), body under 150 words, sign-off with the member's name. Setup note: in the email tool, make a sequence, paste the five emails, set them a day or two apart, connect it to the form, put the PDF link in email 1.

## 6. Test before you post (tick-box list)
- Comment from a second account triggers the DM (your own comment will not).
- The DM link opens the form on a phone.
- Sign up with your own email; email 1 arrives with the PDF link; email 2 arrives on schedule.

## 7. Weekly scorecard
Table, one row per week, 8 empty rows:

| Week of | Posts out | Keyword comments | DMs sent | Sign-ups | Email 1 opens | Email 5 clicks | Bookings |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Leak guide:

| If you see | The likely leak | Fix this week |
|---|---|---|
| Lots of views, almost no comments | The hook or keyword is not clear | Rewrite the hook and put the keyword on the image |
| Comments but few DMs | The automation is broken | Test again from a second account |
| DMs but few sign-ups | The link or form is not working | Open it on your phone |
| Sign-ups but weak opens | The subject lines | Change the subject lines |
| Opens but no bookings | Email 5 is weak | Tighten email 5 or make the first step clearer |

Rules: read left to right, the first stop that drops hard is the leak, fix one thing a week, judge after a month. Improve the magnet itself by changing the title or cover only, or by adding a short section where email 2 replies show people got stuck. Once a quarter read the whole magnet and swap in the best new proof. Make a second magnet only for a second offer for a different buyer. The member's scorecard day goes at the top of this section.
